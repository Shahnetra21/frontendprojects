About Us

Welcome to Krishna Paints Industries, your trusted partner in high-quality paint solutions. We specialize in providing a wide range of paints and coatings for various applications, ensuring durability and aesthetic appeal.

We are a team of experienced professionals dedicated to providing high-quality paint solutions for both residential and commercial projects.

Established in 2004 in Ahmedabad,Gujarat,Krishna Paints Industries has emerged as a leading Wholesaler and Distributor of a wide range of high-quality paints and coatings. Our product line includes Icon Synthetic Enamel Paint, Cement Primer, Super Acrylic Distemper Paint, and many more.With a strong commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, we have built a trusted name in the paint industry, serving clients across various sectors with reliable and affordable solutions.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional paint solutions that exceed customer expectations and contribute to the success of every project we undertake.
